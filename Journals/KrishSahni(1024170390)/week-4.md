# Week 4 Project Journal — Distributed Performance Testing Platform

## MST Week — Agile Workflow and Sprint-Based Planning

### Objective for the Week

The objective of Week 4 was to study Software Engineering concepts for the MST, with particular attention to software development workflows, Agile principles, and the organisation of project activities into sprints.

### Software Engineering Theory

- Reviewed the major stages of the Software Development Life Cycle.
- Studied the Waterfall model and its phase-by-phase development process.
- Explored Agile development as an iterative and incremental approach.
- Examined how sprint planning, task prioritisation, progress reviews, and feedback contribute to organised development.
- Studied how Extreme Programming (XP) supports frequent delivery and continuous improvement.

### Sprint Workflow Planning

The team discussed how LoadMeter could be divided into smaller development increments rather than implemented as one large system.

Potential work areas include the cloud gateway, load generation, local-agent communication, request forwarding, and performance metrics.

Based on the theoretical study and team discussion, the team finalized an **Agile, XP-inspired workflow organised into sprints**. Each sprint is intended to have a defined objective, prioritised tasks, implementation activities, and appropriate testing and review.

This approach is expected to make progress easier to track and integration issues easier to identify.

### Week 4 Deliverables

- Understanding of Agile principles and iterative development.
- Study of sprint planning and backlog prioritisation.
- Preliminary understanding of how LoadMeter can be divided into manageable work increments.
- Team agreement on a sprint-based XP workflow.
