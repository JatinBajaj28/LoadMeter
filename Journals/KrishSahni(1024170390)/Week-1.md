## Objective for the Week

The objective of Week 1 was to understand the load-testing problem, research existing approaches, define the proposed system architecture and features, and prepare the project proposal.

## Day 1 — Problem Understanding

- Researched the fundamentals of load testing and concurrent HTTP request generation.\n- Collected initial technical information required for the project proposal.

## Day 2 — Existing Systems & Research

- Researched performance-testing concepts and important metrics such as RPS, throughput, latency, P50/P95/P99, errors, timeouts, and concurrency.\n- Documented the relevant technical concepts.

## Day 3 — Proposed Architecture / Technical Research

- Studied how telemetry from multiple workers could be aggregated into useful performance metrics.\n- Documented the expected metrics flow for the proposed system.

## Day 4 — Feature & Technical Planning

- Researched configurable SLOs and how they could be used for future performance evaluation.\n- Studied the proposed concepts of adaptive stress testing and breaking-point detection.

## Day 5 — Proposal Development

- Worked mainly on project documentation, technical insights, scope descriptions, and evaluation requirements.\n- Documented proposed future capabilities and their intended purpose.

## Day 6 — Proposal Finalisation

- Consolidated research and documentation for the proposal.\n- Reviewed the written content for clarity, consistency, and completeness before finalisation.
