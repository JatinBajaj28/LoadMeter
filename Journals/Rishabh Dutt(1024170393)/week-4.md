# Week 4 Project Journal — Distributed Performance Testing Platform

## MST Week — Software Development Models and Methodology Selection

### Objective for the Week

The objective of Week 4 was to prepare for the Software Engineering MST, study different software development models, and understand which methodology would be most suitable for the continued development of LoadMeter.

### Software Engineering Theory

- Studied the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) and its major phases, including requirement analysis, design, implementation, testing, deployment, and maintenance.
- Explored the Waterfall model and its sequential approach to software development.
- Studied Agile methodology and its emphasis on iterative development, collaboration, and continuous feedback.
- Compared sequential and iterative workflows in terms of flexibility, planning, and handling changing requirements.
- Studied Extreme Programming (XP) and its emphasis on software quality, frequent feedback, and small releases.

### Methodology Selection for LoadMeter

The team discussed the suitability of different development methodologies for LoadMeter. Since the platform involves multiple interacting components, including the cloud gateway, load generator, local agent, and metrics collection, an iterative development approach was considered appropriate.

The team finalized an **Agile workflow using XP practices, organised into sprints**. This approach will allow the project to be developed incrementally, with each sprint focusing on a manageable set of requirements and their validation.

### Week 4 Deliverables

- Theoretical understanding of SDLC and software development models.
- Comparison of Waterfall and Agile methodologies.
- Study of XP principles and their suitability for LoadMeter.
- Finalized team decision to follow an XP-inspired Agile workflow organised into sprints.
