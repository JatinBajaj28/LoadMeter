# Week 4 Project Journal — Distributed Performance Testing Platform

## MST Week — Extreme Programming and Software Quality

### Objective for the Week

The objective of Week 4 was to study Software Engineering theory for the MST, understand the principles of Extreme Programming, and evaluate how XP practices could support the quality and maintainability of LoadMeter.

### Software Engineering Theory

- Studied the SDLC and the importance of systematic software development.
- Explored the Waterfall and Agile models and their different approaches to planning and implementation.
- Studied Extreme Programming (XP) as an Agile framework focused on feedback, collaboration, and software quality.
- Reviewed XP practices such as test-driven development, pair programming, continuous integration, refactoring, and small releases.
- Examined the role of testing and incremental validation in reducing software defects.

### XP Workflow Finalization

The team discussed how XP principles could be applied to LoadMeter, particularly because the platform involves networking, concurrency, request-response forwarding, and performance measurement.

The team decided to follow an **XP-inspired Agile workflow organised into sprints**. The planned approach emphasises incremental implementation, testing of individual components, and regular review before integrating functionality into the wider system.

Practices such as automated testing, refactoring, and continuous integration will be introduced as appropriate during implementation.

### Week 4 Deliverables

- Theoretical understanding of XP and its engineering practices.
- Study of testing, continuous integration, refactoring, and small releases.
- Identification of quality considerations relevant to LoadMeter's components.
- Team decision to use an XP-inspired sprint workflow for future development.

After studying the different Software Engineering methodologies and discussing their suitability for LoadMeter, **we collectively decided to follow the Agile development approach using Extreme Programming (XP), organised into sprints**. This decision was made through team discussion, considering the need for incremental development, continuous testing, collaboration, and regular review of project progress.
